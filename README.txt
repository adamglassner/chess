CHESS - Ravenscroft Library  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
=========================================

Open Chess from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. Press F11 for full screen.

WAYS TO PLAY
- Play the Computer: choose a strength from Beginner to Master. Someone
  new sits down each game. Play as White, Black or a random colour.
- Same Computer: two players share one PC. Type your names on the title
  screen; the board turns to face whoever is moving (Options can switch
  that off).
- Online: one player chooses Host a Game and tells their friend the
  address on screen. The friend chooses Join a Game and types it in.
  On a home network that's all. Over the internet, the host's router
  must forward TCP port 47025 to the host's computer, and the friend
  uses the host's public address. Both players need the same version.
  Offer a draw, ask to take back a move, or ask for a rematch.
- Optional chess clocks: 5 + 3, 10 minutes, 15 + 10 or 30 minutes.

HOW TO MOVE
- Click a piece and then a square, or drag it. Dots show legal moves,
  rings show captures. Drag the king two squares to castle.
- Right-click a square to mark it, right-drag to draw an arrow.

KEYS
- Ctrl+Z undo, H hint, F flip board, N new game, M music on/off,
  Left/Right arrows step through the moves, Home/End jump to the start
  or the current position, F11 full screen.

The score sheet names the opening and can copy the game as PGN for any
chess program.

Your unfinished game, your record and your settings are saved in
%APPDATA%\Ironite\Chess, outside the install folder, so updating or
reinstalling keeps them.
