CHESS - Ravenscroft Library  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
=========================================

Open Chess from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. The game opens in its own window using Microsoft Edge
(built into Windows 10 and 11). Press F11 for full screen.

PLAY
- Play the computer: choose one of six opponents, from Pip the Page
  (just learning the moves) to the Old Master. Play as White, Black or
  a random colour.
- Two Players: pass and play on one computer. The board turns to face
  whoever is moving (you can switch that off in Options).
- Optional chess clocks: 5 + 3, 10 minutes, 15 + 10 or 30 minutes.

HOW TO MOVE
- Click a piece and then a square, or drag it. Dots show legal moves,
  rings show captures. Drag the king two squares to castle.
- Right-click a square to mark it, right-drag to draw an arrow.

KEYS
- Ctrl+Z undo, H hint, F flip board, N new game, M music on/off,
  Left/Right arrows step through the moves, Home/End jump to the start
  or the current position.

The score sheet names the opening and can copy the game as PGN for any
chess program.

Your unfinished game, your record against each opponent and your
settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\Chess, outside the install
folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

All pieces, boards, portraits, music and sounds are original and made
by the game.
