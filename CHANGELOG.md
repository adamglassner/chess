# Changelog

All notable changes to Chess are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added
- Play Together online: one player hosts and a friend joins by IP address, over the internet or on your home network, the same way as Texas Hold'em, Go Fish, Old Maid and Euchre. Offer draws, ask to take back a move and ask for a rematch with colours swapped. Your online record is kept.
- Same Computer games now use the players' names on the plaques, the score sheet and the result.
- The computer opponents get a fresh name every game, one that fits their portrait and their place in the library (Mrs. Ashby, Brother Bede, Judge Blackwood, Professor Adebayo...). The six strengths are the same as before.
- The setup adds a Windows Firewall rule so hosting an online game works without a prompt.

### Changed
- Chess is now a native Windows game written entirely in Go. It no longer opens in a Microsoft Edge window, starts faster and runs in its own window (F11 for full screen).
- The pieces, boards, portraits, music and sounds are the same as before, drawn at your screen's full resolution.
- Your record against each opponent is now shown by strength (Beginner, Casual, Club player, Strong, Expert, Master).
- Your unfinished game, record and settings carry over from 1.x in %APPDATA%\Ironite\Chess.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- First release of Chess for Windows.
- Full rules of chess: castling, en passant, promotion to any piece, check, checkmate and stalemate, plus draws by threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material.
- Play the computer or a friend. Six members of the Ravenscroft Library will give you a game, from Pip the Page (just learning the moves) through Mrs. Hollis, Brother Ambrose, Judge Whitlock and Professor Vance to the Old Master.
- Play as White, Black or a random colour, with or without a chess clock (5 + 3, 10 minutes, 15 + 10 or 30 minutes).
- Click-to-move or drag-and-drop, with dots for legal moves, last-move and check highlights, and a piece picker for promotion.
- Undo, Hint, Flip Board, Offer Draw and Resign. Right-click to mark squares and right-drag to draw arrows while you plan.
- A score sheet in standard notation that names the opening, lets you step back through the game and copies the moves as PGN.
- Your unfinished game is waiting when you come back, and your record against each opponent is kept, in %APPDATA%\Ironite\Chess.
- HD graphics drawn at your screen's full resolution, from 720p up to 4K: hand-modelled ivory and ebony Staunton-style pieces, and three boards to choose from (Walnut & Maple, Marble and Club Green) on a leather-topped library desk.
- Four original chamber pieces: "The Quiet Library", "Nocturne of the Long Diagonal", "Minuet in the Reading Room" and "Endgame by Lamplight", with volume control and mute.
- Sound effects for wooden pieces on the board, captures, castling, check, promotion and the end of the game.
